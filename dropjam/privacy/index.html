Drop Jam — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Drop Jam game for Android and iOS (Android package com.itsartstudio.dropjam), made by ItsArt Studios (“we”, “us”). It explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • No account or sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • No advertising and no in-app purchases.
  • We collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to understand how the game is played and to fix problems.
  • We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising.

Information we collect

When you play, the following is collected automatically through the Google Firebase services listed below:

  • an app instance identifier generated by Firebase, and the Android advertising ID where your device makes it available to Firebase Analytics;
  • device information: device model, operating system version, screen size, language and app version;
  • approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address — never precise GPS location;
  • how you use the game: sessions, levels played and completed, in-game settings and similar gameplay events;
  • crash reports: the state of the app and device when a crash or error happens, and an installation identifier.

Your game progress and settings are stored on your device only.

How we use it

  • to understand which parts of the game work well and which are too hard or confusing;
  • to find and fix crashes and bugs;
  • to keep the game running reliably.

We do not use this information to identify you personally, to show you ads or to build an advertising profile.

Third-party services

The game uses these services provided by Google LLC, which process the data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — anonymous usage statistics (Firebase privacy information)
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and error reports

See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. Besides the service providers above, we disclose information only if required by law, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

Data retention

Analytics data is kept no longer than 14 months; crash reports are kept for 90 days. After that they are deleted automatically.

Your choices

  • You can stop all collection at any time by uninstalling the game.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device’s Google settings.
  • You can ask us to delete the data associated with your installation, or ask what we hold, by writing to itsart.developer@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

Children

Drop Jam is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and is stored by Google Firebase with industry-standard safeguards.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are published on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

Questions about privacy in Drop Jam: itsart.developer@gmail.com.
