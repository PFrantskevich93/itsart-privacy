Need help with Bouquet Jam, found a bug or have an idea? Write to us at itsart.developer@gmail.com — we usually answer within two business days.

To help us fix a problem faster, please mention your device model, its OS version and, if you can, the level where it happened.

Common questions

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically on your device. Deleting the game also deletes the saved progress.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No, you can play offline.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No.

How do I ask for my data to be deleted?

Email us from the address above; see the privacy policy for what is collected.
